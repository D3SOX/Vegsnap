# Reviewed company concerns

`company-concerns.json` is a small reviewed dataset, shipped for local/offline lookup. Eligible categories are animal testing, documented opposition to animal-welfare protections, and other documented animal exploitation. Selling animal products alone is never a qualifying concern. Absence from this dataset is not ethical clearance. AI does not create misconduct records.

Records need a stable company ID, qualified English/German descriptions, source URL, source date if known, review date, current/resolved/disputed status, and redistribution notes. Summaries are original factual descriptions under AGPL-3.0-only; linked source documents retain their own copyright and are not bundled. Ownership links have separate source and review dates. Review both conduct and ownership before updating a record; mark resolved/disputed records accordingly instead of silently deleting history.

Initial coverage: Nestlé’s published February 2017 animal-testing policy, reviewed on 2026-10-06. It permits specified regulatory/research testing, requires alternatives where possible, and excludes development of conventional human foods/drinks and cosmetics. This does not establish testing of a scanned burger. Sources:

- [Nestlé animal-testing policy](https://www.nestle.com/asset-library/Documents/Library/Documents/Corporate_Social_Responsibility/nestle-policy-animal-testing.pdf)
- [Nestlé’s Hälsans Kök ownership statement](https://www.nestle.dk/da/media/pressreleases/garden-gourmet-bliver-til-hlsans-kk)
- [Garden Gourmet in Nestlé’s brand portfolio](https://www.nestle.com/brands/culinary-chilled-frozen/garden-gourmet)

Matching uses explicit brand metadata from user input, product databases, or AI product identification. It matches whole normalized names/curated aliases, rejects ambiguous aliases, and follows one reviewed parent link. Product titles and ingredient text are not searched for company names. Identity can still be mistaken; users should check the matched brand shown in the result. Company concerns remain separate from product classification and certification. No online company lookup or user-history contribution occurs.

Coverage is intentionally limited, not an exhaustive corporate ethics directory. There is no automatic periodic review or assertion that an old source proves a new incident: source dates and the last review are visible. New records require source review; do not add unsupported allegations to fill a category.
