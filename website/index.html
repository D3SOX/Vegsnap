Download the Linux x86_64 archive and extract it somewhere permanent. Use the included Python installer to register the companion for your browser.

Download Linux companion↓

For macOS and other architectures, build from source. Installation commands and Windows instructions.

Register for Firefox

After extracting to ~/.local/lib/veguide:

python3 ~/.local/lib/veguide/install.py \
  --binary "$HOME/.local/lib/veguide/veguide-companion" \
  --browser firefox \
  --extension-id veguide@veguide.app

For Chromium, use --browser chromium and the ID on chrome://extensions. Then open the extension's settings and choose Connect ChatGPT.

Linux needs an unlocked Secret Service wallet. Credentials stay in your operating system's credential store.

A few things to know

Is Veguide free?

Yes. Veguide is free and open source. Public database checks and local rules need no AI account. Optional AI providers have their own usage limits and charges.

Does it work for every product?

You can check any product category, including clothes and shoes. Some products have too little information for an answer. Veguide then shows what is missing.

Can I use my ChatGPT plan?

Eligible accounts can connect ChatGPT as an optional AI provider. The browser extension uses a small desktop companion for this connection. API-key connections work without the companion.

Where does product information come from?

Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts provide community product data. The Android Browse tab also searches Barnivore for drinks and Wikidata for product information. Downloadable regional packs contain a partial selection of product records. Supported AI connections can research web sources. Community data and AI-read claims are not independent certification checks.

How are company concerns selected?

Veguide shows reviewed records of animal testing, opposition to animal-welfare protections and other documented animal exploitation. Connected AI can also assess the company using searched sources. Its assessment is labeled separately and may be inconclusive. Parent-company concerns need ownership evidence. Neither changes the product's vegan result. Selling animal products alone does not qualify, and no concerns found is not an ethical endorsement.

What if the evidence is incomplete?

Veguide explains what is missing. When AI finds a manufacturer contact, you can copy a question about the product or open an email draft to ask for clarification.

What about iPhone?

A native iOS app is planned for later. This release focuses on Android and desktop browsers.
