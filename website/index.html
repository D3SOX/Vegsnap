THE EVIDENCE

See what supports
the result

A shoe's material list may omit its glue. Vegsnap points out missing details and distinguishes ingredient checks from vegan claims on a label.

  • ✦
    Certified vegan

    Product-specific certification evidence supports it.

  • ▣
    Manufacturer says vegan

    A matching statement from the maker supports it.

  • ✓
    Composition appears vegan

    The supplied ingredients or materials support it.

  • ×
    Animal-derived content found

    See the specific ingredient or material.

  • ?
    More information needed

    Missing details and conflicting sources stay visible.

Local history

Vegsnap saves your history on your device. You can delete saved checks and photos.

Optional AI

Connect a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, an API provider or a compatible local model. Checks you start may send selected text and photos to that provider.

No background AI

Store integrations use product databases in the background. AI runs when you start a check.

Open source

The app, extension and companion use the AGPL-3.0 license. They have no ads or analytics.

Install the companion

Download the archive for your operating system to your Downloads folder. Install Python 3, then run these commands in a terminal. Keep the extracted files in this location.

Linux↓Windows↓macOS↓
Linux

For Linux x86_64. Credentials need an unlocked Secret Service wallet, such as KDE Wallet with Secret Service enabled.

mkdir -p "$HOME/.local/lib/vegsnap"
tar -xzf "$HOME/Downloads/vegsnap-companion-linux-x86_64.tar.gz" \
  -C "$HOME/.local/lib/vegsnap"
chmod +x "$HOME/.local/lib/vegsnap/vegsnap-companion"
macOS

For Intel and Apple Silicon. The companion uses macOS Keychain. It is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, review the executable in System Settings under Privacy & Security.

mkdir -p "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Vegsnap"
tar -xzf "$HOME/Downloads/vegsnap-companion-macos-universal.tar.gz" \
  -C "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Vegsnap"
chmod +x "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Vegsnap/vegsnap-companion"
Windows

For Windows x86_64. Install the Visual C++ v14 Redistributable for x64, then run these commands in PowerShell. The companion uses Windows Credential Manager.

$vegsnapDirectory = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA "Vegsnap\Companion"
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force -Path $vegsnapDirectory | Out-Null
Expand-Archive -Force `
  -Path "$HOME\Downloads\vegsnap-companion-windows-x86_64.zip" `
  -DestinationPath $vegsnapDirectory

A few things to know

Is Vegsnap free?

Yes. Vegsnap is free and open source. Public database checks and local rules need no AI account. Optional AI providers have their own usage limits and charges.

Does it work for every product?

You can check any product category, including clothes and shoes. Some products have too little information for an answer. Vegsnap then shows what is missing.

Can I use my ChatGPT plan?

ChatGPT sign-in on Android and in the browser extension requires a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Free ChatGPT accounts are not supported for this connection. The browser extension uses a small desktop companion for this connection. API-key connections work without the companion.

Where does product information come from?

Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts provide community product data. The Android Browse tab also searches Barnivore for drinks and Wikidata for product information. Downloadable regional packs contain a partial selection of product records. Supported AI connections can research web sources. Community data and AI-read claims are not independent certification checks.

How are company concerns selected?

Vegsnap shows reviewed records of animal testing, opposition to animal-welfare protections and other documented animal exploitation. Connected AI can also assess the company using searched sources. Its assessment is labeled separately and may be inconclusive. Parent-company concerns need ownership evidence. Neither changes the product's vegan result. Selling animal products alone does not qualify, and no concerns found is not an ethical endorsement.

What if the evidence is incomplete?

Vegsnap explains what is missing. When AI finds a manufacturer contact, you can copy a question about the product or open an email draft to ask for clarification.

What about iPhone?

A native iOS app is planned for later. This release focuses on Android and desktop browsers.
