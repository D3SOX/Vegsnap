Vegsnap AI offers limited free checks without a subscription or API key. Selected product text and photos pass through Cloudflare to OpenAI. You can also connect your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, an API provider or a compatible local model.

Connect ChatGPT in your browser

The desktop companion connects the extension to your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Vegsnap AI, API-key connections and Android ChatGPT sign-in work without it.

  1. Install the companion

    Download the companion for your operating system and open it. Choose the browsers where you use Vegsnap, then select Install. Setup copies the companion into your user folder, so you can delete the download afterwards. It does not need administrator access.

    Linux Windows macOS
    Linux

    For Linux x86_64. Extract the archive and open vegsnap-companion. ChatGPT credentials need an unlocked Secret Service wallet, such as KDE Wallet with Secret Service enabled.

    macOS

    For Intel and Apple Silicon. Open the archive, then open Vegsnap Companion. Apple has not notarized the app, so macOS may block it the first time. Choose Open Anyway in System Settings under Privacy & Security. The companion stores credentials in the macOS Keychain.

    Windows

    For Windows x86_64. Open the downloaded file. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognized app. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The companion stores credentials in Windows Credential Manager.

  2. Connect ChatGPT

    Open the extension's settings, choose Connect ChatGPT and allow the requested permission. Complete sign-in in your browser.

    The companion supports these browsers:

    • Google Chrome
    • Brave
    • Helium
    • Vivaldi
    • Microsoft Edge
    • Zen
    • LibreWolf

Questions about Vegsnap

Is Vegsnap free?

Yes. Vegsnap is free and open source. Database checks and local rules need no AI account. Vegsnap AI offers limited free checks. Other cloud AI providers have their own usage limits and charges.

How do free AI checks work?

In Settings, choose Vegsnap AI and select Connect to free AI. Complete verification in your browser, then return to Vegsnap to see your remaining allowance. You need no subscription, API key or desktop companion. Access expires after 24 hours and allowances reset at midnight UTC. A shared service limit can make free checks unavailable. A failed AI request can count as a check. Selected product input passes through Cloudflare to OpenAI. Read the privacy policy for storage and retention details.

Does it work for every product?

You can check any product category, including clothes and shoes. Some products have too little information for an answer. Vegsnap then shows what is missing.

Can I use my ChatGPT plan?

ChatGPT sign-in on Android and in the browser extension requires a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Free ChatGPT accounts are not supported for this connection. Android connects directly and lets you save and switch between ChatGPT accounts in Settings. The browser extension needs the desktop companion. Vegsnap AI and API-key connections work without it.

Where does product information come from?

Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts provide community product data. The Android Browse tab also searches Barnivore for drinks and Wikidata for product information. Downloadable regional packs contain a partial selection of product records. Vegsnap AI, ChatGPT and the official OpenAI API can research web sources. Other compatible API connections do not currently support web search. Community data and AI-read claims are not independent certification checks.

How are company concerns selected?

Vegsnap shows reviewed records of animal testing, opposition to animal-welfare protections and other documented animal exploitation. Connected AI can also assess the company using searched sources. Its assessment is labeled separately and may be inconclusive. Parent-company concerns need ownership evidence. Neither changes the product's vegan result. Selling animal products alone does not qualify, and no concerns found is not an ethical endorsement.

What if the evidence is incomplete?

Vegsnap explains what is missing. When AI finds a manufacturer contact, you can draft a question in English, German or Swedish. Copy it or open an email draft to ask for clarification.

Can I read manufacturer replies?

Look up reviewed replies in the Android app or open the community service from the extension. To contribute a reply, use the submission form and redact personal details before sending it. Approved replies stay separate from the product verdict. Opening a product result does not upload your history.

What about iPhone?

The latest GitHub release includes the native iPhone and iPad app as an unsigned IPA. Sign it yourself before installing it. Sign the share extension too, and give both the App Group entitlement so they can share data. It needs iOS or iPadOS 18 or later and is not on the App Store.
