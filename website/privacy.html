Vegsnap privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Android and iOS apps, browser extension and optional desktop companion

Vegsnap checks ingredients, materials and product evidence. You do not need a Vegsnap account. The app and extension have no ads, analytics or telemetry. Saved history stays on your device. Vegsnap does not send your personal provider credentials to its maintainers. If you choose Vegsnap AI, selected product input passes through the maintainer-operated service to OpenAI.

Android app

The Android app has no ads, analytics or telemetry. Camera images are processed on your device unless you explicitly start a check with a configured AI provider. Imported photos use Android's system picker; Vegsnap does not request access to your entire photo library. Text recognition uses bundled or optionally downloaded Tesseract language models on your device.

Settings, queued checks, history and attached photos are stored in the app's private storage. Delete individual checks or clear history in the app to remove saved results and their photos. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage; files you exported remain until you delete them. Android backup and device-to-device extraction of app data are disabled. Blocking a community reply saves only its reference on your device; you can restore blocked replies in the community section.

API keys and ChatGPT credentials are encrypted with keys held in Android Keystore. ChatGPT sign-in and model discovery exchange authentication and account information with OpenAI directly; Android does not require the desktop companion. Disconnect ChatGPT or remove the API key in Settings to remove the corresponding stored credentials. Provider-side accounts and data must be managed with that provider.

Online product lookups send barcodes, product or brand search terms and market codes to the relevant services, including Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts, Wikidata and Barnivore. Language-model downloads and regional-pack downloads contact their listed hosts, including GitHub. These services receive your IP address and normal request information. Catalogue and database queries do not attach camera frames, check history or AI credentials.

When you enable optional AI and start a check, Vegsnap may send selected text, prepared photos and product details to the provider you choose. Prepared photos are re-encoded to remove image metadata before transmission. Providers may perform web research using product identifiers and may retain inputs and outputs under their own policies. API providers can charge for requests; connecting ChatGPT uses your eligible plan. Remote endpoints use HTTPS; a configured local model may use unencrypted HTTP on loopback. Offline mode stops new online checks.

Opening a product automatically checks reviewed community replies. It sends only the product's barcode, name, brand and country to the community service on Cloudflare. Android offline mode prevents this request. Saved scan contents, photos, history and provider credentials are not sent. Matching whole-product replies can update the displayed verdict without rewriting the saved scan. Sharing opens its contribution form in your browser. No reply, photo or history is uploaded merely by opening the form. Manufacturer, evidence and support links use external services with their own privacy policies.

On Android, ChatGPT tokens are encrypted using Android Keystore. Saved account registrations retain the email from a verified sign-in locally so you can identify accounts after disconnecting. Account labels and credentials are excluded from Android backups and are not sent to Vegsnap's maintainers. Sign-in diagnostics contain only fixed failure-stage and error classifications.

On Android, explicit checks automatically use the selected AI connection when it is ready and local or database evidence cannot answer. Disconnect the provider to stop using it, or enable Offline mode to prevent all network requests. Connecting a ChatGPT account selects an available model; you can change it in Settings.

iOS app

The iPhone and iPad app has no ads, analytics or telemetry. Barcode scanning and text recognition use Apple's Vision framework on your device. The system photo picker gives Vegsnap access to photos you select, rather than your entire library. Photos prepared for checks are resized and re-encoded to remove image metadata.

Settings, drafts, queued checks, history, attached photos, imported offline packs and blocked community-reply references are saved in the app's private files, which are excluded from system backups. You can clear a draft, remove queued or saved checks, clear history, remove packs or restore blocked replies in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app files; exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them.

The share extension copies only text, links and images you choose to share into a local inbox shared with Vegsnap and excluded from system backups. Sharing does not start an online check. The app imports the content into a draft for review and sanitizes its photos before use. Inbox entries are removed after import; failed imports or entries that cannot be removed may remain locally until the app is deleted.

API keys, ChatGPT tokens and saved account registrations, and Vegsnap AI access tokens and installation identifiers are stored in the iOS Keychain, accessible while the device is unlocked and restricted to this device. The app does not enable iCloud Keychain synchronization. Keychain entries can remain after deleting the app: remove API keys, disconnect providers and remove saved ChatGPT accounts in Settings before deletion if you want to remove those credentials. Disconnecting ChatGPT removes its active tokens; saved account registrations, including the email used to identify an account, remain until you remove them. The anonymous Vegsnap AI installation identifier remains after disconnecting to preserve free-use limits.

ChatGPT sign-in, token refresh and model discovery communicate directly with OpenAI; iOS does not use the desktop companion. Checks you start can send entered text, selected prepared photos and product details to the selected AI connection when local evidence cannot answer. Disconnect the selected provider or enable Offline mode in Settings to stop using it. Provider retention policies and usage limits apply. Remote endpoints use HTTPS; a local model can use unencrypted HTTP on loopback.

Online barcode and product searches send identifiers or packaging-based product and brand search terms to the product services described below. Pack downloads contact their listed hosts, including GitHub. These services receive ordinary network information, including your IP address, but catalogue and database requests do not include photos, history or AI credentials. Opening a product result automatically looks up reviewed community replies on Cloudflare using only its barcode, name, brand and country. iOS Offline mode prevents this request. Saved scan contents, photos, history and provider credentials are not sent. Matching whole-product replies can update the displayed verdict without rewriting the saved scan. Contribution forms and evidence links open external services; opening a contribution form does not upload a reply or saved history. In-app content reports and optional Vegsnap AI checks use the services and retention rules described below.

Browser storage and companion credentials

Settings, imported offline product packs, saved results and attached photos stay in this browser's local extension storage. History is enabled by default; you can disable future saving, delete individual checks or clear your history. Exporting history creates a file on your device. Uninstalling the extension removes its browser storage; exported files and the separately installed companion remain until you remove them.

API keys are stored in browser session storage and cleared when the browser closes. The optional companion stores ChatGPT credentials in your operating system's credential store. The extension may temporarily display account status and your email address; it keeps connection status and the model list in session storage.

Online product databases

When AI identifies a Swedish food or drink product from a photo, Android, iOS, the browser extension using a direct OpenAI API connection, and the Vegsnap AI service may search Matspar's public catalogue using the product name and brand read from the packaging. Matspar receives these search terms and ordinary network request information, including the IP address of the device or hosted service making the request. Catalogue queries do not include photos, check history or AI credentials.

When a barcode is not in the partial offline data, Vegsnap queries Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts. Requests contain the barcode and application identifier. Those services also receive your IP address and ordinary network request information. They do not receive your check history, photos or AI credentials. Their own privacy policies apply.

Optional website features

Selected text and product information are read when you use the toolbar or context menu. Shop integrations are off by default. Enabling a supported shop grants access to that shop and allows automatic barcode lookups. Background browsing never calls AI and does not save checks to history.

Importing a remote product image requires permission for its host. The image host receives the image URL, your IP address and normal request information; Vegsnap omits credentials and the referrer for this download. You can import a saved photo instead.

Optional AI and the desktop companion

You choose whether to configure an AI provider. For checks you start, when local rules and databases cannot answer, Vegsnap can send your selected or entered text, photos, product name, brand, source-page URL, category, market and language to that provider. A source URL can reveal which product page you visited. API-key requests authenticate with your configured provider. Local model requests go to the local endpoint you configure.

ChatGPT connections in the browser extension use the separately installed companion. Sign-in and model discovery exchange account and authentication information with OpenAI; product checks send the selected check input to the companion and then to OpenAI. Connected providers may use product details for web research. Provider retention policies, terms, charges and usage limits apply. Never include unrelated personal information in product input.

Vegsnap does not run AI during background browsing. Disconnect the companion, choose "Databases only", clear your API key or revoke endpoint permissions to stop using a provider. Companion credentials and any provider-side data must be managed separately through the companion, the operating system or your provider.

Optional Vegsnap AI checks

The optional Vegsnap AI connection in Android, iOS and the extension uses a maintainer-operated Cloudflare Worker that forwards your selected product input to OpenAI using the maintainer's API key. You do not need a ChatGPT subscription or personal API key. Turnstile verification in a browser helps prevent abuse. Cloudflare receives your IP address, ordinary network information and browser verification data. Vegsnap sends a temporary session token to this service to authenticate requests; it does not send your personal provider credentials or saved history.

The application database stores hashes of the session token and an anonymous installation identifier, verification state, expiry and usage counters. The installation identifier stays on your device so reconnecting does not reset your daily free allowance; it is separate from your temporary access token. The database does not save product text, photos, AI responses, email addresses or IP addresses. Unverified connections expire after ten minutes; verified sessions expire after 24 hours. Expired records and old daily counters are deleted hourly. Cloudflare's backup and security-data retention still applies. Application request logs are disabled. OpenAI receives the selected product input and may use product details for a limited web search; its API retention policies apply even though response storage is disabled.

The extension keeps the token in browser session storage until you disconnect or close the browser. Android stores it encrypted in its private settings until you disconnect; iOS stores it in the device-only Keychain. Reopening either app checks whether the session is still valid. Disconnect removes local access and attempts to revoke the server session; any unrevoked session expires automatically. Free checks have session and shared service limits. A failed AI request may consume its allowance. You can choose database-only checks or another provider at any time. Read Cloudflare's privacy policy and OpenAI's API data controls for their handling of data.

Firefox consent and permissions

In Firefox, online content sharing, source-page URLs, authentication information and account information are declared as optional data permissions. Vegsnap requests the relevant permissions when you start an online feature. Declining or revoking them prevents the corresponding network or native-companion requests; local ingredient checks and offline product data remain available. Manage these choices in Firefox's extension "Permissions and data" settings. Browser host permissions and native messaging are also requested for the features that use them.

Community manufacturer replies

In the browser extension, opening a product automatically looks up reviewed replies on Cloudflare using only its barcode, name, brand and country. Firefox data-sharing permission is required before this lookup. Community buttons also open the contribution or lookup page in a browser tab. Product identity and language are prefilled through the URL fragment, which is not sent in the initial web request. The community page can use that identity to search replies or prefill its form. Neither the lookup nor these links include your saved result contents, photos, credentials or history, and no referrer is sent. A reply can cover several products or a moderator-reviewed product range and specified countries. Range-name suggestions require you to confirm that the product belongs to the range before changing its displayed verdict. Matching whole-product confirmations may update the verdict; ingredient-only confirmations do not. Contradictory evidence remains visible. The service receives ordinary network information when you visit it. Opening a contribution form does not submit a manufacturer reply. Only content you deliberately submit in that form is uploaded; redact personal details before submission. Reviewed contributions may be published by that service.

Content reports

The Android and iOS reporting forms let you report AI output or a community reply without leaving the app. Review and redact the editable AI text and your reason before sending. AI reports send only that text and your reason; community reports send only the reply reference and your reason. Photos, saved history and provider credentials are not attached. Reports go to Vegsnap's moderation service on Cloudflare, where they are private and accessible only to the maintainer. Cloudflare receives ordinary network information; IP addresses are used transiently for rate limiting and are not saved in the report database.

The maintainer uses reports to investigate objectionable content, withdraw inappropriate community replies and improve AI safeguards. Reports are deleted when handled, or expire after 30 days and are removed by the next hourly cleanup. Do not include personal information in a report. For a privacy or deletion inquiry, use the support contact below and provide the report reference without reposting its private text.

Use and sharing

Vegsnap uses information only to provide the product-checking features described here. It does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. It shares information only with the services you use for these features. Information received from Google APIs is used in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

Links and third-party services

Opening evidence, manufacturer, support, community or download links takes you to a third-party service governed by its own privacy policy. The Vegsnap website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives normal web request information. The site uses no analytics, tracking cookies or remote fonts.

Data deletion and contact

To request deletion of a community contribution or content report, contact the maintainer by email: . Include its reference and say which data you want removed. Do not send credentials or repeat private report text. The maintainer can remove the contribution or report from Vegsnap's service. Approved contributions remain published until withdrawn. Pending submissions expire after 30 days and rejected submissions after 7 days. Reports also expire after 30 days. Delete local history and photos in the app, and disconnect Vegsnap AI in Settings to revoke its temporary session. Expired sessions and old daily usage counters are removed hourly. For data held by an AI provider or another external service, use that provider's deletion controls; its retention and infrastructure-backup policies apply.

Vegsnap is maintained by D3SOX. For privacy or deletion questions, contact the maintainer by email: . For public bug reports, use the project support page. Do not post credentials or private product-check data in a public issue. Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
